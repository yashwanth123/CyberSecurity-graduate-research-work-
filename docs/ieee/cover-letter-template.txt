Please find our submission entitled:

  "An Open IEC 62443 Assessment Framework for Manufacturing IACS: 
   Design and Empirical Case Study"

We present an open-source assessment platform that operationalizes IEC 62443 
for manufacturing IACS, combining compliance checklists, Security Level analysis, 
zones/conduits modeling, and risk registration. A case study at an anonymized 
automotive assembly line demonstrates 49.1% compliance maturity, SL gaps at 
DMZ/Operations zones, and four critical OT risks.

We believe this work fits the workshop themes of novel methods for ICS/OT security, 
OT supply chain exposure, and cloud/MES integration into operational technology.
